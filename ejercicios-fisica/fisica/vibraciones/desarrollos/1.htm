$$\left.\begin{aligned}&x=A\operatorname{sen}(\omega\,t+\varphi_0)\\[4pt]&v=A\,\omega\cos(\omega\,t+\varphi_0)\end{aligned}\right\}$$
$$\left.\begin{aligned}&\frac{x}{A}=\operatorname{sen}(\omega\,t+\varphi_0)\\[4pt]&\frac{v}{A\,\omega}=\cos(\omega\,t+\varphi_0)\end{aligned}\right\}$$
$$\left.\begin{aligned}&\frac{x^2}{A^2}=\operatorname{sen}^2(\omega\,t+\varphi_0)\\[4pt]&\frac{v^2}{(A\,\omega)^2}=\cos^2(\omega\,t+\varphi_0)\end{aligned}\right\}\;\rightarrow\;\frac{x^2}{A^2}+\frac{v^2}{(A\,\omega)^2}=1$$
$$v^2=(A\,\omega)^2\left(1-\left(\frac{x}{A}\right)^2\right)$$
$$v=A\,\omega\sqrt{1-\left(\frac{x}{A}\right)^2}$$
