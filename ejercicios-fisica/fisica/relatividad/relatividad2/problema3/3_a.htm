Energía relativista del electrón en reposo: E0 = m0.c2

Energía relativista del electrón en movimiento: E = m·c2

Conversión de masas: m = m0/γ

Combinando estas tres expresiones resulta un sistema con incógnitas: m0, m, E

$$\begin{aligned} &\left.\begin{aligned}E_0&=m_0\,c^{2}\\[4pt] E&=m\,c^{2}\\[4pt] m&=\frac{m_0}{\gamma}\end{aligned}\right\}\\[10pt] &\boxed{E=\frac{E_0}{\gamma}\quad,\quad m=\frac{E_0}{\gamma\,c^{2}}} \end{aligned}$$

$$\begin{aligned} &\gamma=\sqrt{1-\left(\frac{v}{c}\right)^{2}}=\sqrt{1-\left(\frac{0{,}8 c}{c}\right)^{2}}=0{,}6\\[6pt] &E=\frac{E_0}{\gamma}=\frac{0{,}51\ \mathrm{MeV}}{0{,}6}=0{,}85\ \mathrm{MeV}\\[6pt] &m=\frac{E_0}{\gamma\,c^{2}}=\frac{0{,}51\ \mathrm{MeV}}{0{,}6\,(3\cdot10^{8}\ \mathrm{m/s})^{2}}=\frac{0{,}51\cdot10^{6}\cdot1{,}6\cdot10^{-19}\ \mathrm{J}}{0{,}6\,(3\cdot10^{8}\ \mathrm{m/s})^{2}}=1{,}51\cdot10^{-30}\ \mathrm{kg} \end{aligned}$$
