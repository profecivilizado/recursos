$$\left.\begin{aligned}&\frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\,\frac{Q_1}{r_1}+\frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\,\frac{Q_2}{r_2}=0\\[4pt]&r_1+r_2=d\end{aligned}\right\}\xrightarrow{(1)}r_2=-\frac{Q_2}{Q_1}\,r_1$$

$$r_2=-\frac{Q_2}{Q_1}\left(\frac{d}{1-Q_2/Q_1}\right)=\frac{d}{1-Q_1/Q_2}$$
