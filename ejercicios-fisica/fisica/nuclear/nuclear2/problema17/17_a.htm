$$\begin{aligned} &A=\lambda\,N=\frac{1}{\tau}\,N\;\rightarrow\;N=A\,\tau\\[8pt] &N=3{,}7\cdot10^{9}\ \mathrm{\frac{1}{s}}\ 8{,}04\cdot24\cdot3600\ \mathrm{s}=2{,}57\cdot10^{15}\ (\text{átomos}) \end{aligned}$$
